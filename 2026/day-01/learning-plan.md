# My learning plan for 90 Days 
- Weekly 6 days learning - 1 day revision
-2 hours in weekdays
-4 hours on weekends
-3 posts on linkedin every week

Weekdays (2 Hours)
60 min → Learn (videos / reading)
45 min → Practice (hands-on / exercises)
15 min → Notes + quick recap

Weekends (4 Hours)
2 hrs → Build / Project work
1 hr → Revise entire week
1 hr → Create content (LinkedIn posts)

(3 LinkedIn Posts)
Post 1 (Learning Insight)
“This week I learned…”
Share 1 concept in simple terms

Post 2 (Practical/Build)
Show what you built or practiced
Include screenshots or results

Post 3 (Reflection/Story)
Challenges, mistakes, lessons
